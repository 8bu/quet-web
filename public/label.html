Keyboard and mouse

Complete needs every target marked

enter (save as complete) is refused while a span that applies to the chosen type is still unmarked. Click or drag the text to mark it, or press n if there is none. u (uncertain) and s (skipped) are never blocked.

This is a deliberate difference from the Quet terminal annotator, which would quietly save an unmarked target as null — easy to do by accident when you forgot to mark it.

Offsets are counted in Unicode characters (code points), so an emoji counts as one. The end is exclusive: "nước dùng" [22,31) covers characters 22 to 30.

Press ? or esc to close.
