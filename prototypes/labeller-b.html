Prototype notes — B · queue + detail

Design bet. People who label hundreds of records a day want to see the pile shrink. The whole queue stays on screen on the left: each row shows its text, the label you gave it and a tick; unlabelled rows show a ◇ when they have a proposal. The right pane is a dense form for the current record. Filtering the list (f or the chips) lets you sweep “uncertain” records again, and every record is one click away. Less breathing room than A, more orientation.

Try: click rows · f to cycle the filter · save a record and watch the tick land · p on rv-001, rv-004, rv-009 · rv-006 has an invalid proposal.
