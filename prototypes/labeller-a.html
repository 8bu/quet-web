Prototype notes — A · focus card

Design bet. One record at a time, the text is the hero. Everything you can do to a record is on one small palette to the right, always in the same place, so your eyes stay on the sentence. The card says what to do next (the grey hint line) and a record you already saved shows its saved status, so revising feels the same as labelling. A proposal sits in a separate dashed “not accepted” block, never mixed into the draft.
